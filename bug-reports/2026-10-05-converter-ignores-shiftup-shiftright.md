# Level converter ignores the original's `shiftRight` / `shiftUp` half-tile offsets (211 enemies 8 px off), and lifts are anchored by their left edge instead of their centre (139 of 204 lifts 4-16 px off)

- **Severity:** wrong behaviour
- **Build:** V0.1.0-B8379F9
- **Where:** level data from `tools/levelgen/convert-smbc.mjs` and spawn placement in `src/game/world/world.ts` (`makeEntity`) and `src/game/entities/objects/lift.ts`, both map sets (85 levels). Examples: ll-2-3, red Paratroopa at column 65, row 11 (8 px low); 1-1, Goombas at columns 52, 98, 115 and 175 (8 px left); ll-7-2, lifts at columns 21 and 28 (8 px right) next to the one at 44 (right); 1-3 and 3-3 sideways lifts (16 px right).
- **How to get there:** `?level=ll-2-3&char=mario&dev=1`, walk to column 60 with F1 on and watch the red Paratroopa over the gap at columns 63-66. For lifts, `?level=ll-7-2&char=mario&dev=1`, columns 21-44.
- **Character and power:** any
- **Input:** keyboard (scripted through Playwright)
- **Browser and device:** headless Chromium on Linux (cloud container)

## Steps

1. Pick an object marked `shiftRight` or `shiftUp` in the original's XML. In ll-2-3 (`<LEVEL ID="2-3">`), cell 65,11 is `enemyWingedKoopaRed&&shiftUp`. In ll-7-2, the lift at 44,11 is `movingPlatform&&shiftRight&&width=4&&type=WaveHorizontal`, while the lifts at 21,10 and 28,10 have the same width and type but no `shiftRight`.
2. Compare where our game places it (F1, or the `.map` entity line / grid marker) with where the original's code places it (below).

## Expected

**Shift tokens.** `com/smbc/level/Level.as` lines 791-794 set `shiftRight` / `shiftUp` from each `()`-separated item (`PROP_SHIFT_RIGHT`, `PROP_SHIFT_UP`, lines 129-130; both reset per item, lines 733-734). Lines 1254-1257 then apply them to every object built from the cell: `if (shiftRight) objectToSpawn.x += TILE_SIZE*.5; if (shiftUp) objectToSpawn.y -= TILE_SIZE*.5;`. That is 16 Flash px = **8 px at our scale**. Before that, enemies (any `AnimatedObject`) are anchored at the cell's bottom centre (`x = currentX + TILE_SIZE/2`, `y = currentY + TILE_SIZE`, lines 1249-1251). Special cases: `playerStart` adds its own half tile (lines 613-621), `halfwayPoint` uses `currentX + TILE_SIZE/2` instead of `currentX` (lines 1069-1072), and `bowserFireBallStart` ignores the token (`bfbX = currentX`, line 1153).

**Lift anchor.** A lift (`Platform`, which is `Ground`, not an `AnimatedObject`) is placed at `x = currentX + TILE_SIZE/2`, `y = currentY` (`Level.as` lines 1205-1207), then shifted as above. Its hit box is the `HRect` child of the platform clip, read in `Platform.setColPoints` (`hMidX = this.x`). In the game file (`MovieClipInfo_PlatformMc`) that `HRect` is centred on the clip's origin in every width frame: `normal-2` x -16..16, `normal-3` -24..24, `normal-4` -32..32, `normal-6` -48..48 Flash px, top at y 0. So **the original centres every lift on the centre of its cell** (on the cell's right edge with `shiftRight`), with its top on the cell's top (half a tile higher with `shiftUp`).

Causes:
- **Tokens dropped:** `parseCell` in `tools/levelgen/convert-smbc.mjs` (lines 51-67) keeps `shiftRight` / `shiftUp` in `params`, but nothing reads them. There is no `shift` anywhere in `tools/levelgen/`. Every object is written at its cell.
- **Enemies** are centred on their cell by `makeEntity` (`src/game/world/world.ts` lines 282-330, e.g. `new Goomba(x + px(2), y + px(2))`, `new Koopa(x + px(2), y - px(6), …)`; every body is 12 px wide), the same as an unshifted original enemy, so a shifted one is 8 px left and/or 8 px low.
- **Lifts** are anchored by their left edge: `Lift` (`src/game/entities/objects/lift.ts` line 30) does `super(px(tx * 16), px(ty * 16), len * 8, 8)`, and the converter passes the XML `width` straight through as `len` (`convert-smbc.mjs` lines 531-543). So our lift's centre is at `tx*16 + len*4`; the original's is at `tx*16 + 8` (+ 8 with `shiftRight`). The difference is `len*4 - 8 - (shiftRight ? 8 : 0)` px: 0 only for the 65 width-4 lifts with `shiftRight`. Width-3 lifts are 4 px right, unshifted width-4 lifts 8 px right, width-6 lifts 16 px right (8 with `shiftRight`), and `shiftUp` lifts are also 8 px low.
- **Already right**, because our runtime builds them around the 2-tile object rather than the cell: Piranha Plants are centred on their pipe (`piranha.ts` lines 46-47, comment "the original's shiftRight"); Bowser's 28 px body at `tx*16+2` is centred at `tx*16+16` (`bowser.ts` line 36); pipe entry needs Mario inside both pipe columns (`world.ts` lines 1180-1183) and pipe-exit starts are centred on the pipe (`world.ts` lines 189-192); checkpoints respawn centred in the tile, which is the original's shifted point (the trigger is within 2 px).

**Fix approach** (reviewer's suggestion):
1. In the converter, read `params.shiftRight` / `params.shiftUp` and emit a pixel offset prop (for example `dx=8`, `dy=-8`) on the entity lines of enemies, lifts, `pitTransferEnd` and stand-type starts. Skip Piranha Plants, pipes, Bowser, `halfwayPoint`, `bowserFireBallStart` and scenery text, which are already right or decorative.
2. Apply the offset in `makeEntity` and `Lift`. Don't write fractional tile coordinates instead: `tileToSub` is `t << TILE_SUB_SHIFT` (`src/engine/math/units.ts` line 33), so `52.5` would be truncated to 52.
3. Centre lifts on their cell: left edge `tx*16 + 8 - len*4` (+ 8 with `shiftRight`), top `ty*16` (- 8 with `shiftUp`). This also moves the swing centre of sideways and vertical swinging lifts (see Notes) and touches every lift test in `src/content/levels/`.
4. Rerun the converter and update the map tests.
