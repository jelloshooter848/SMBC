- **Severity:** blocks progress
- **Build:** V0.1.0-B8379F9
- **Where:** ll-12-4, the one-tile shaft at column 29 (rows 5-10). It drops into the lava at columns 29-33, row 13. The sideways lift is at column 31, row 13. ll-7-4 has the same start section, shaft and lift. ll-11-4 has the same trap at column 64 (lift at column 66, row 10). Both were confirmed in play (see Notes).
- **How to get there:** `?level=ll-12-4&char=mario&dev=1`, then go right along the top corridor (rows 3-4) to its end at column 29. Faster: a share-link test map of `ll-12-4.map` with only `start:` moved to `28,4`, the step just left of the shaft (`session-scratchpad/gauntlet/notes/llwC/t124-28.url`). ll-7-4: `?level=ll-7-4&char=mario&dev=1`, same route. ll-11-4: `?level=ll-11-4&char=mario&dev=1`, then right along the corridor at rows 3-5 to column 63.
- **Character and power:** Mario, small. In ll-12-4, Dev assists No damage and Inf. lives were on, only to get past the fire bars at columns 16 and 22. Lava still kills. The ll-7-4 and ll-11-4 retests used no assists.
- **Input:** keyboard (scripted through Playwright)
- **Browser and device:** headless Chromium on Linux (cloud container)

## Steps

1. In ll-12-4, reach column 28, row 4, at the end of the first corridor. The row 2 ceiling is solid from column 0 to 43, and columns 30-43 are wall from row 3 down to row 10. So the only way on is down the one-tile shaft at column 29.
2. Walk right into the shaft and keep holding right while you fall.
3. Repeat at different points in the lift's cycle.

## Expected

The lift is the only way across the lava, so it has to pass under the shaft. In the original it swings under column 29:

- **Data:** `levelDataLostLevels.xml`, `<LEVEL ID="12-4">`, area a. Cell 31,13 is `wavesLava | movingPlatform&&width=4&&type=WaveHorizontal`, with no `shiftRight`. The lava (`wavesLava`) is at columns 29-33, row 13, with ground again from column 34. The shaft at column 29 is open from row 3 to row 12. Column 28 is ground from row 5 down. Column 30 is ground from row 3 to row 10. (Column 30 is `WideCharacter=Hide`, so wide characters get a 2-tile shaft. Mario gets a 1-tile one.)
- **Placement:** `com/smbc/level/Level.as` line 1207 places a platform at `x = currentX + TILE_SIZE/2`, the centre of column 31 (31.5 tiles). The platform's hit box (`HRect`) is centred on that point, so the width-4 lift covers 30.5 to 32.5 at rest. That part was checked against the game file by the converter review (`2026-10-05-converter-ignores-shiftup-shiftright.md`).
- **Swing:** `com/smbc/ground/Platform.as` lines 173-177 set `centerX = x` for `WaveHorizontal`. Lines 316-323 move it with `x = centerX + sin(waveAngle) * hWaveRange`, where `hWaveRange = 60` and `hWaveSpeed = 1.5` (lines 58-59). With 32 px Flash tiles, 60 px is ±1.875 tiles.
- **Result:** the lift's left edge swings from column 28.6 to 32.4. It is left of column 30, so it covers part of the shaft column, for about 1.7 s of every 4.2 s cycle. At the left end it covers 28.6-30.6, the whole shaft column.

## Actual

Our lift (`ll-12-4.map` line 33: `lift-h 31 13 len=4 range=3`) never goes left of column 31. `src/game/entities/objects/lift.ts` (`update`, case `'lift-h'`, lines 63-70) moves a `lift-h` on a triangle path between `originX` and `originX + range`. Here that is the left edge of column 31 (x 496) and x 544, at 1 px per frame (96-frame cycle). So the 2-tile lift covers x 496-576, columns 31-36. The shaft is x 464-480, and its right wall (column 30) ends at x 496.

While Mario is in the shaft, holding right only pushes him into the wall, which zeroes his speed every frame (`src/game/entities/body.ts` lines 45 and 55). He can only start drifting right once his head is below row 10. By then his feet are 16 px above the lift's top. He would need at least 16 px of rightward drift in that fall. Falling at about 4 px/frame, with air acceleration of 0.037 px/frame² from a standing start (`src/game/characters/mario/profile.ts`), he drifts about 1 px. He lands in the lava.

Results (tester):
- Test map, `start: 28,4`. The tester walked into the shaft holding right (and right+run) at 18 points in the lift's 96-frame cycle: six starts 350 ms apart and twelve starts 133 ms apart. Mario died every time at x 474, tile 29,15, in the lava.
- Real level from its start: playing right through the first corridor, Mario dropped down the same shaft into the lava at column 29.

Screenshot (screenshot not committed: the repo's `check:assets` bans image files) (the test map, so the HUD reads WORLD 1-1): Mario has just fallen into the lava at column 29. The lift is near the left end of its path, at columns 31-33.
